# Full geopolitical evolution — approved

Autonomous factions evaluate neighbors, negotiate treaties, create alliances, exchange resources, collect imperfect intelligence and select foreign-policy strategies.

## Modules
relations, treaties, alliances, trade_routes, negotiations, intelligence, foreign_policy, diplomatic_ai, geopolitical_simulation.

## Design
Diplomatic decisions depend on trust, historical relations, trade, security, culture and rulers. Intelligence is incomplete. Military, economic and cultural interactions must remain bounded and work in distant abstract regions.
