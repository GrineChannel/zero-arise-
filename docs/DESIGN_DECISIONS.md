# Approved ZERO: Arise design decisions

- Hybrid Minecraft world with settlements, cities, gates and fantasy zones.
- Autonomous NPC needs, professions, families, social relationships and inheritance.
- Configurable aging: children 4x; adults 1x; elders 1x; players off; NPCs on.
- Autonomous relationships; mixed inheritance model.
- Full civilization simulation including states, laws, economy, diplomacy and warfare.
- Full technology evolution, industry and magitech.
- Dynamic modular magic: spells, runes, rituals, mechanisms, NPC research.
- Magic consequences: instability, anomalies and environmental effects.
- Magic safety: limited block destruction; anomaly spread enabled; protected player territories; normal disaster frequency.
- Full magical evolution, mutations, population dynamics and ecosystems.
- World day target: 120 real minutes.

The existence of a design decision does not mean the corresponding mechanic is implemented.
