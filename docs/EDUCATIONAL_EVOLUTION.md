# Full educational evolution — approved

Civilizations autonomously establish schools, vocational workshops, universities, magical academies, scientific institutes, military academies and libraries. NPCs learn, teach, conduct research and pass knowledge to later generations.

## Modules
institution_catalog, institutions, curricula, learning, teaching, research_institutes, knowledge_transfer, access, education_simulation.

## Design
Education affects scientific advancement, magic, industry, social mobility and history. Access depends on institutional capacity, cost, distance and policy. Archives and teachers help preserve knowledge; wars and catastrophes may cause losses. Research discoveries use authored technology and magic primitives rather than dynamic engine code.
