# Destruction, disasters, ruins and recovery — approved

The world simulates structural damage, fire, sieges, flooding, earthquakes, drought, mana anomalies, NPC evacuation, refugees, ruins and repair.

## New pure modules
damage, hazards, spread, evacuation, ruins, recovery, displacement, disaster_simulation, safety.

Limited block destruction, anomaly spread enabled, protected player territories, normal catastrophe frequency. Distant regions are abstract and never directly edit unloaded chunks.
