# Autonomous cultural evolution — approved

Cultures emerge and change through history, migration, contact, trade, catastrophe, philosophy, beliefs and social movements. Culture is not fixed by species, and NPCs retain individual outlooks.

## Modules
values, traditions, philosophy, beliefs, movements, diffusion, individual_beliefs, cultural_events, cultural_simulation.

## Design safeguards
Belief claims are representations of what NPCs believe, not proof of supernatural truth. Values are descriptive simulation parameters, not moral rankings. Cultural contact is gradual and bounded. Events change values in small steps.

## Integration remaining
Connect to NPC relationships, organization membership, diplomacy, history, education, world clock and persistence. Add movement outcomes, ideological disagreement, rituals, seasonal festivals and performance tests. Modules are abstract and do not yet affect Bedrock entities.
