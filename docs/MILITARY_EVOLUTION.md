# Autonomous military evolution — approved

Civilizations recruit armies, develop equipment, choose doctrines, train commanders, maintain logistics, conduct sieges and select military strategies.

## Modules
unit_catalog, armies, equipment, doctrines, logistics, battle, sieges, commanders, strategy, military_simulation.

## Simulation
Loaded regions may eventually use real Bedrock NPCs. Distant regions use bounded aggregated battle calculations. Battle outcomes depend on force composition, morale, experience, terrain, weather and doctrine. War interacts with diplomacy, economy, destruction and history.

## Remaining integration
Connect armies to faction treasuries, technology and equipment inventories, movement routes, settlement defenses, diplomacy, casualties and recruitment. Add persistence, detailed siege results, tactical NPC AI and Bedrock entity spawning. Current modules are abstract and have not been playtested.
